Questions

Is 55300 free?

Yes. Every calculator, table and guide is free with no sign-up. The site is funded by advertising, reader support and clearly labelled sponsorships.

Are the tax numbers current?

Yes. Calculations use the IRS 2026 brackets and standard deduction and the 2026 Social Security wage base. Last reviewed October 21, 2026.

Do you store what I type?

No. Calculators run entirely in your browser. Only forms you choose to submit (like the Pay Check-Up) are sent to us.

Why is the site called 55300?

$55,300 is a useful benchmark for a typical full-time American salary — and our domain. Read why 55300.
