Support FAQ

Is my donation tax-deductible?

No. 55300 is an independent website, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

How do I pay?

Use any payment button shown. If a button says 'pledge', submit the pledge form and we'll reply with a secure payment link — we never ask for card details by email or form.

Can I give monthly?

Yes — choose 'Monthly' in the pledge form and we'll set up a recurring link you can cancel anytime.

Can my company sponsor instead?

Yes. Sponsorships include visible, clearly-labelled recognition. See Advertise & sponsor.
